Task 2: User Research and Persona Development
In Task 2, I conducted user research through surveys and interviews, gathering valuable insights about the target audience’s preferences and pain points. I utilized this data to create user personas, which helped in understanding the various user groups and their specific needs. By focusing on these personas, I was able to tailor the design to meet the demands of each segment effectively.
The research process also involved analyzing competitors' designs to identify areas of improvement and opportunities for differentiation. This helped in making informed design decisions, ensuring that the app’s interface would stand out while solving real-world problems for users.
